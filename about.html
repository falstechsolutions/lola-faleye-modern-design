About Lola

Beauty entrepreneur, cultural architect, and the force behind Africa's most influential beauty platforms.

The Story

20+ Years of Redefining Beauty

Omolola Faleye is a seasoned Beauty & Events entrepreneur with over 20 years of experience across various industries. She holds a Bachelor's degree in Accounting from the University of Abuja, a certification in Innovation and Strategy from Harvard Extension School, and a Women's Entrepreneurship Certification from Bank of America Institute at Cornell University.

She is a member of the International Association of Exhibitions and Events, ForbesBLK and PremiaBN. Her entrepreneurial journey began when she resigned from her IT/ICT Project Management position to become the brand representative for Sleek — earning recognition as the Best PR Distributor for Nigeria in 2008 and becoming Managing Partner for Sleek Franchise Abuja in 2009.

Philosophy

Commerce Meets Culture

Her work sits at the intersection of commerce, culture, and community — building platforms that don't just participate in the beauty industry, but redefine it. From product formulation to event production, from brand consulting to industry thought leadership — her journey has been one of relentless evolution.

Passionate and committed, Omolola continuously invests in professional development, completing courses in Business Management, Digital Marketing, Creative Enterprise, and Project Management. As a dedicated mentor, she kindles the flame of ambition in individuals, encouraging them to pursue their dreams.

Author

AYD Power Planner

Omolola is the author of the influential Power Planner AYD — a comprehensive resource designed to facilitate goal planning and execution. This self-guided planner provides a structured system for daily, weekly, monthly, and quarterly planning, empowering individuals to manifest their goals and fast-track the realization of their dreams.
