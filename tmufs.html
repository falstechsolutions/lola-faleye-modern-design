The MakeUp Fair Series — Nigeria's largest makeup and beauty trade show.

Overview

Where the Beauty Industry Meets

The MakeUp Fair Series (TMUFS) is a cross-national industry event that tours major cities in Nigeria, creating a unique marketplace where beauty brands, makeup artists, and consumers converge. It's more than a trade show — it's where deals are made, trends are set, and the next generation of beauty professionals finds its footing.

The Bigger Picture

TMUFS isn't just about showcasing products — it's about building an ecosystem. By connecting emerging brands with established players, and consumers with authentic beauty experiences, TMUFS is laying the groundwork for a self-sustaining beauty economy across Nigeria and beyond.
