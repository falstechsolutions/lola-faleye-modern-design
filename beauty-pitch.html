Beauty Pitch Africa

Changing the narrative and disrupting the future of the African beauty industry.

Overview

Building an African Beauty Community

Beauty Pitch Africa is a platform aimed at building an African beauty community that will help change the narrative and disrupt the future of the African beauty industry. Through mentorship, investment connections, and community-driven events, BPA empowers emerging beauty entrepreneurs to scale their businesses across the continent.

Mission

Why This Matters

The global beauty market is valued at hundreds of billions — yet African beauty brands remain underrepresented. Beauty Pitch Africa exists to bridge that gap by providing the mentorship, capital connections, and community infrastructure that African beauty entrepreneurs need to compete on the world stage.
